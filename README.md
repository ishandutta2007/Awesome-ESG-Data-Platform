# Awesome-ESG-Data-Platform

## Top ESG Data Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on ESG Data Management, Carbon Accounting, Sustainability Reporting & Climate Intelligence*  

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **ESG Data Platforms**. These tools collect, normalize, govern, analyze, and report environmental, social, and governance data across corporate operations, supply chains, climate programs, and regulatory disclosures.



**Examples** include Datamaran, Workiva ESG, Diligent ESG, Novisto, Persefoni, IBM Envizi, Sweep, Watershed, Greenly, and FigBytes, alongside broader sustainability and carbon-management platforms such as Sphera, Microsoft Sustainability Manager, Normative, Position Green, and Plan A.



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with open-source projects, climate-data infrastructure, emissions-accounting libraries, ESG data models, environmental datasets, and analytical tooling that can be combined to build self-hosted ESG data platforms.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites or project repositories.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[OS-Climate Community Hub](https://github.com/os-climate/OS-Climate-Community-Hub)**  

  Linux Foundation ecosystem for open-source climate and sustainability data infrastructure, analytics, physical-risk analysis, transition analysis, portfolio alignment, and ESG data tooling.



- **[OS-Climate Data Commons](https://github.com/os-climate/os_c_data_commons)**  

  Open climate-data infrastructure designed to provide standardized access to financial, ESG, geospatial, emissions, and climate datasets.



- **[OS-Climate Financial Entity Cleaner](https://github.com/os-climate/financial-entity-cleaner)**  

  Open-source tooling for cleaning and matching financial entities, useful for normalizing corporate ESG and sustainability datasets.



- **[OS-Climate ESG Matching](https://github.com/os-climate/esg-matching)**  

  Open-source entity-matching tooling focused on connecting and reconciling ESG-related corporate data.



- **[OS-Climate Portfolio Analysis](https://github.com/os-climate/)**  

  Open-source climate-finance analytics ecosystem for portfolio alignment, transition analysis, and climate-related investment intelligence.



- **[Open Footprint Forum](https://github.com/OpenFootprintForum)**  

  Open-source ecosystem focused on standardized carbon-accounting data, methodologies, schemas, and tooling for emissions measurement.



- **[Open Footprint Data Model](https://github.com/OpenFootprintForum)**  

  Community-driven work toward interoperable data structures for representing carbon footprints and emissions activity data.



- **[Climatiq](https://github.com/climatiq)**  

  Developer-oriented carbon-intelligence ecosystem providing APIs, emissions-factor data, and tooling for embedding emissions calculations into software.



- **[Carbon Interface](https://github.com/carbon-interface)**  

  Developer-focused carbon-emissions calculation ecosystem that can be used as a building block for custom carbon accounting applications.



- **[PUDL — Public Utility Data Liberation](https://github.com/catalyst-cooperative/pudl)**  

  Open-source data engineering project that cleans and integrates public energy-system datasets, useful for energy, emissions, and climate analytics.



- **[Catalyst Cooperative](https://github.com/catalyst-cooperative)**  

  Open-data and open-source ecosystem for energy-system datasets and analytical infrastructure that can support environmental and climate intelligence.



- **[OpenGHG](https://github.com/OpenGHG)**  

  Open-source ecosystem around greenhouse-gas data, measurement, and analysis.



- **[GHG Protocol Tools & Data Projects](https://github.com/search?q=GHG+Protocol+carbon+accounting&type=repositories)**  

  Community repositories implementing GHG Protocol calculations, emissions factors, reporting workflows, and carbon-accounting utilities.



- **[GLEIF Data](https://github.com/finos)**  

  Open financial-entity infrastructure that can be incorporated into ESG data pipelines to normalize corporate legal entities and ownership structures.



- **[Frictionless Data](https://github.com/frictionlessdata)**  

  Open-source standards and tooling for describing, validating, transforming, and exchanging structured datasets — useful for ESG data pipelines.



- **[Great Expectations](https://github.com/great-expectations/great_expectations)**  

  Open-source data-quality framework that can be used to validate ESG datasets, emissions measurements, supplier submissions, and reporting data.



- **[Apache Airflow](https://github.com/apache/airflow)**  

  Open-source workflow orchestration platform suitable for building automated ESG data ingestion, transformation, validation, and reporting pipelines.



- **[Dagster](https://github.com/dagster-io/dagster)**  

  Open-source data orchestration framework well suited to building governed ESG data pipelines and asset-based sustainability data workflows.



- **[dbt Core](https://github.com/dbt-labs/dbt-core)**  

  Open-source analytics-engineering framework for transforming raw sustainability and ESG datasets into documented analytical models.



- **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)**  

  Open-source business-intelligence platform suitable for building ESG dashboards, sustainability KPI monitoring, and environmental reporting interfaces.



- **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)**  

  Open-source BI and analytics platform that can provide self-hosted ESG dashboards and reporting on sustainability datasets.



- **[Grafana](https://github.com/grafana/grafana)**  

  Open-source observability and visualization platform that can be adapted for environmental, energy, emissions, and ESG KPI monitoring.



- **[Eclipse Dataspace Components](https://github.com/eclipse-dataspace-components)**  

  Open-source components for interoperable data spaces, potentially useful for exchanging ESG and supply-chain sustainability data between organizations.



- **[Trino](https://github.com/trinodb/trino)**  

  Open-source distributed SQL query engine suitable for querying large federated ESG, climate, financial, and environmental datasets.



- **[DuckDB](https://github.com/duckdb/duckdb)**  

  Open-source analytical database particularly useful for local ESG data processing, emissions datasets, sustainability reporting pipelines, and exploratory analysis.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Carbon Accounting Libraries** for Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 emissions calculations.

- **GHG Protocol Implementations** for automated emissions calculations and reporting workflows.

- **Open Emissions-Factor Databases** for activity-based carbon accounting.

- **Open Climate Risk Models** for physical climate-risk and transition-risk analysis.

- **Open Supply-Chain Data Tools** for supplier ESG data collection and normalization.

- **Open ESG Data Schemas** for interoperable sustainability KPI and disclosure data.

- **Open Energy Data Projects** for electricity generation, consumption, grid emissions, and renewable-energy analytics.

- **Open LCA / Life-Cycle Assessment Projects** for product and process environmental-impact analysis.

- **Open Materiality Tools** for double-materiality assessment and ESG issue prioritization.

- **Open ESG Reporting Templates** for GRI, SASB, ISSB, CSRD/ESRS, CDP, and TCFD-aligned reporting.

- **Open Geospatial Climate Tools** for flood, heat, wildfire, drought, and other physical climate-risk analysis.

- **Open Entity Resolution Tools** for linking ESG data from sustainability reports, corporate registries, financial databases, and supplier datasets.

- **Open NLP / Document Extraction Tools** for extracting ESG metrics and disclosures from annual and sustainability reports.



**Frameworks for building custom ESG platforms**: Combine **OS-Climate**, **Open Footprint**, **Climatiq**, **PUDL**, **DuckDB**, **dbt Core**, **Airflow/Dagster**, **Great Expectations**, **Trino**, and **Superset/Grafana** to build a self-hosted ESG data platform covering ingestion, entity resolution, emissions accounting, data quality, analytics, and reporting.



## How to Contribute



1. Fork the repo.

2. Add/edit entries in `README.md` (follow existing format).

3. Include: name, link, 1–2 sentence description, and whether it's SaaS or open-source.

4. Prioritize genuinely open-source projects with an accessible repository or open license.

5. Submit PR with a short explanation.



Star the repo if you find it useful!



## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- ESG reporting requirements and applicable disclosure frameworks vary by jurisdiction and organization.

- SaaS capabilities, product names, ownership, and licensing models can change over time.

- Some projects listed under the open-source ecosystem are **building blocks rather than complete ESG platforms**; they may require integration with additional databases, emissions-factor datasets, analytics tools, or reporting systems.

- Always verify the applicable license before using open-source components commercially.

- ESG calculations and disclosures should be validated against the applicable reporting standards, accounting methodologies, and assurance requirements.
